Every transcription is kept in the history, on the left of the window, together with its summary, its translation and the corrections you made. The button on the left of the top bar shows or hides it.

## Find a transcription

Type in **Search** to find the transcriptions whose name, text, note, tag or source contain the words, ignoring the case.

Each row shows the name, the date, the kind of source (**File**, **URL**, **Microphone**, **Folder** or **Watched folder**) or its tag, and its status if it isn't done: **Queued**, **Watching**, **Failed**, **Cancelled** or **Interrupted** (when Audiotext was closed before it finished).

## Organize it

Right-click a transcription to:

- **Rename** it.
- **Add note…** or **Edit note…**: a note about it, shown in its header.
- **Edit tag…**: a short label shown instead of the kind of source.
- **Pin to the top** or **Unpin** it.
- **Move to group**: put it in a group, or create a **New group…**.
- **Show in Finder** (or Explorer, or the file manager): open the folder of its source file.
- **Delete…** it.

### Groups

Groups gather related transcriptions, such as the ones of a project or a course. Create one with **New group** at the top of the history, or from **Move to group**. Right-click a group to **Rename group…** or **Delete group…**. Deleting a group keeps its transcriptions, without a group.

## Delete a transcription

Deleting a transcription removes it, its summary and its translation from the history. The transcriptions of the files of a folder are deleted too. Your audio, video and saved files are **not** deleted.

## Failed and interrupted transcriptions

When a transcription fails, is cancelled or is interrupted, its page shows why and lets you **Try again** or **Delete** it.

## Where it's stored

The history is stored in your user configuration folder, with the recordings of the microphone. See [Files and data](/en/reference/files-and-data/).
